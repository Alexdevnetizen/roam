## Reproducing the treatment

The set was unified with a single grade (lifted blacks, rolled-off highlights, warmed midtones and
highlights, cooled shadows, reduced saturation, light sharpening) and cropped to fixed aspect
ratios with `ffmpeg`. Originals were fetched from Commons at `width=1800` via
`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/<file>?width=1800`.
