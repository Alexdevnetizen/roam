# PRODUCT.md — ROAM

## What this is

ROAM is a **fictional premium travel brand**, built as a portfolio-quality frontend study. One landing page, no backend, no commerce. Its job is to read as a real boutique travel house: high-craft art direction, restrained interactions, and honest communication about what is and is not connected.

## Audience and scene

People who travel for how a place feels, not for a checklist. They read print magazines, keep a paper notebook, and are put off by urgency and discount language. They will open this on a phone, on a commute, and it must feel calm there too.

## Positioning and promise

Curated travel for the quietly adventurous. Discover meaningful destinations and extraordinary escapes. Tagline: **"The world, a little closer."**

Voice: concise, evocative, thoughtful, human. No clichés, no exaggerated promises, no urgency.

## Mode

**Persuade.** The page earns attention and a click into the destinations, then a click on "Find your escape". It is judged on whether a first-time visitor understands what ROAM is, believes it has taste, and knows what to do — within seconds.

## Surface inventory

One scroll: Nav, Hero, Philosophy, Destinations (3), Curated Escapes (3), The ROAM Difference (4 principles), Travel Journal (3 pieces), Closing CTA, Footer with client-validated newsletter.

Real interactions required: destination note overlays, journal reading overlays, swipeable/scrollable escapes gallery, mobile menu, keyboard paths on every control.

## Truth constraints

- No invented testimonials, awards, partnerships, statistics, prices, or booking availability. Nothing is for sale.
- No fabricated customer names in photographs' attribution, no fake press logos.
- Photography is sourced and credited in `public/images/CREDITS.md`.
- The newsletter is not connected to any service and must say so rather than implying a subscription happened.
- No dead controls: no `href="#"`, no placeholder buttons.
- Social links lead to the platforms themselves, not to accounts that do not exist; the footer says so in small print.

## Out of scope

Auth, database, API, payments, bookings, admin UI, real newsletter delivery, React Router unless a second route is genuinely needed.
